# 06. Environmental Evidence Assessment

## Purpose
Ensure ecological interpretation respects empirical boundaries: elevated leaf asymmetry is a non-specific morphological signal that does **not** uniquely identify air pollution.

## Evidence & Confounder Checklist
1. **Confounders to Record & Discuss:**
   - **Edaphic/Soil factors:** pH, organic matter, mobile metal availability, moisture (Adamovich & Olkova 2024 observed FA 0.064–0.076 with zero pollutant exceedance).
   - **Climatic & Microhabitat:** June/growing-season temperature, drought, canopy shading vs open sun.
   - **Biotic stressors:** previous-year and current-year insect herbivory, pathogens.
2. **Prohibited Causal Phrasing:**
   - Never output *"air pollution is high"* or *"clean air confirmed"* solely from leaf $Z$.
   - Replace with neutral statements: *"elevated relative morphological asymmetry index; verify DA/AS/ME and environmental covariates (soil, microclimate, herbivory, anthropogenic load)"*.
3. **Configurable Classification Scale:**
   - Keep threshold-based scores (e.g. Zakharov 5-point scale) **optional and toggleable**, disabled by default until local reference baselines and statistical assumptions (DA/AS/ME) are met.

## Rejection Criteria
- **Reject** any report or UI badge that equates single-leaf asymmetry or unvalidated site $Z$ directly with atmospheric pollution severity.
